## Tower Pixel Dungeon (Pixel Towers), ported 2026-10-16 (key `tower`, repo FixAkaTheFix/Tower-Pixel-Dungeon)

A tower defense mod, so most of its 445 new classes need waves, a gold currency, towers or its mode window. What stands on its own is in.

In. 12 worlds from its tile sheets (Tower Sewers, Oozy Sewers, Tower Prison, Tower Temple, Tower Caves, Goblin Grotto, Tower Mines, Tower City, Dwarven Gates, Mages' Quarter, Tower Halls, Soul Desert), regions picked from where its arenas use each sheet (`scripts/tower-tiles.py` builds the sheets and fills missing cells from Shattered's). Three of its songs (goblin grotto, gnoll grotto, metropolis) play in Goblin Grotto, Tower Mines and Mages' Quarter. 23 monsters in `ModRoster` (Gnoll Blind, Thrower, Twilight, Goblin Fat, Giant, Ninja, Sand, three Goblin Shamans, Hermit Crab No Shell, Licteria Lasher (weight 0), Magi Crab, Slugger, Armored Skeleton and Shielded one, Timeless Spirit, Chief Rat and five Camp Rats plus the Leader). Camp Rats were the hero's allies in the mod, here they are enemies. 15 weapons (Banhammer, Berserker Axe, Blue Knife, Bone Machete, Darksteel Saber, Dual Hatchet, Golden Sword, Infipike, Iron Whip, Molten Dagger, Pike, Plague Scourge, Red Knife, Stone Hammer, Trisai), the curses Degrading and Unreliable, the glyph Holy, the enchantments Empowering and Pure, Ring of Lethality, Wand of Snakes, Potion of Immortality, Scroll of Drowsiness, Scroll of Discoveries. Weapons and items are in the journal catalog only, not in the loot tables.

Shims added to core, do not write again. `Char.speak(text, color)`, `CharSprite.BLACK` and `MYSTERIOUS`, `Mob.ranged`, `Mob.targetingPreference` (kept, it does not change target choice), `MeleeWeapon.rarity`, `critChance`, `critMult` (a hit is multiplied now and then, only for weapons that set a chance) and `damageModifier()`, `PathFinder.NEIGHBOURS25`. `renames.py` rewrites the 3.x duelist ability calls (`abilityChargeUse(hero)`, `heavyBlowAbility`, `cleaveAbility`, `sneakAbility`, `beforeAbilityUsed`). `DamageSource` is a slim version with only classes from this game, used by the monsters that resist magic or poison. Stand-ins. Protected and Strength buffs use the Armor and Momentum icons, Blue Knife uses the Red Knife picture (its own cell was empty), Gnoll Thrower, Magi Crab, Shielded Armored Skeleton and Chief Rat use the mod's own sheets in `sprites/tower/` because they have extra frames.
