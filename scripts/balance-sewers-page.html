Sewers balance check

Floors 1 to 5 of seeded runs, built the way a normal game builds them. Each monster and item counted was on the floor when it was made. A monster's hit is 400 rolls of its normal attack. Zaps, explosions and other special attacks are not included.

Damage times chance is the average hit multiplied by the chance the monster is on a floor. It shows how much each monster adds to the danger of a typical sewer floor. Click any column to sort.

Items

Flat damage is the weapon's average hit as found (its upgrade level included), with the lowest and highest roll under it. Damage times chance is that average multiplied by the chance the item is on a floor. Items that are not weapons have no damage.
